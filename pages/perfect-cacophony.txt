<p><strong>Perfect Cacophony</strong> is an extremely difficult max mode from "Sister Location Super Custom Night", which is a fangame based on the "Five Nights at Freddy: Sister Location" custom night, featuring 8 new animatronics and quality of life improvements from the base game. The night features 18 unique animatronic threats that you must survive until 6am, progressively getting more difficult throughout the night, with an intense climax at 5am including visual changes, a flashing countdown, and auditory distractions. It is somewhat known in the [[dashnet-forums-discord-server|DashNet Forums Discord server]] due to the [[perfect-cacophony-meme|meme]] made about it, and [[foxgirlmaki|Maki]] occasionally streaming her attempts to complete it, with over 100 hours of in-game playtime across her journey. It is currently ranked at #20 on the <a href="https://allmodeslist.pages.dev/">All Modes List</a>.
