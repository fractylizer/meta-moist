<p><strong>Bidybab</strong> is one of the main threats featured in [[perfect-cacophony|Perfect Cacophony]]. She attacks through the top vent, instantly charging into her third and final phase as opposed to her base game counterpart, who slowly crawls through your vent. You must watch her on the camera with the vent door closed to push her back and prevent her from killing you. She is often found to be one of the hardest animatronics to deal with throughout the night, due to having to constantly check on her because of her extremely fast movement speed and low kill-timer, causing the player to have significantly less time to deal with the other threats present. This difficulty is what caused her to feature in the [[perfect-cacophony-meme|Perfect Cacophony meme]], made by [[foxgirlmaki|Maki]].
